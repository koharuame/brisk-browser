Get Brisk for Windows 11.

One small download. Setup checks your PC, installs Brisk for your account, and you're browsing in under a minute.

Download Brisk 0.1.0 Brisk_0.1.0_Setup.exe, about 7 MB. Free.

What setup does

  1. Checks your PC

    Windows 11, a 64-bit processor, the WebView2 engine, disk space and memory, with a note on anything worth knowing.

  2. Asks two questions

    Whether you'd like a desktop shortcut, and whether to open Windows' default-browser settings afterwards.

  3. Installs for you only

    Brisk goes into your own app folder, so no admin rights are needed. If WebView2 is missing or out of date, setup gets it.

  4. Opens Brisk

    Brisk is on your Start menu. Bring your bookmarks and passwords over from Settings whenever you like.

If Windows asks before running setup

Brisk's installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box the first time.

  1. Select More info.
  2. Check the app name reads Brisk_0.1.0_Setup.exe.
  3. Select Run anyway.

Only run setup that you downloaded from this site or from github.com/koharuame/brisk-browser.

Questions

Is Brisk free?

Yes. Brisk is free to download and use, with no account and no ads of its own.

How do updates work?

Once a day Brisk asks this site's release page for the newest version number. When there's an update, a dot appears on the settings button; choose Update and restart in Settings → About whenever it suits you.

Every update is checked against Brisk's signature before it's installed. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings.

Can I bring my bookmarks and passwords?

Yes. Import bookmarks from another browser's HTML export, and passwords from the CSV export of Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox, in Settings.

Does Brisk work on Windows 10?

Not yet. Brisk is built and tested for Windows 11 only, and setup stops on older versions of Windows.

Where does Brisk keep my data?

On your PC only, in your user folder under AppData\Roaming\app.brisk.browser and AppData\Local\app.brisk.browser. Nothing is stored online.

How do I uninstall Brisk?

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Brisk, and choose Uninstall. Tick Delete the application data if you also want your history, bookmarks and passwords removed.

Is the source code available?

Not at the moment. This site and its release page are where Brisk is published, and where you can report a problem.
