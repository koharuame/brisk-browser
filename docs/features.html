Everything Brisk does.

A browser should load what you asked for, keep what you didn't ask for out, and stay quiet about the rest. Here's every part of Brisk, and what it does for you.

Ads and trackers, stopped before they load

Blocking happens on every network request, before anything is downloaded, so pages finish sooner and don't jump around as ads arrive.

See the measurements
The lists people trust
EasyList, EasyPrivacy, EasyList Thailand and uBlock Origin's own filter lists, updated in the background every two days. Add any other filter list you like.
Clean pages, not empty boxes
The spaces ads leave behind are hidden too, and the scripts some sites use to fight blockers are neutralized.
Malware and phishing warnings
Brisk checks addresses against URLhaus and phishing lists stored on your PC. No address you visit is sent anywhere to be checked. You can continue to a site anyway if you're sure.
One click to trust a site
The shield in the address bar shows how many requests were blocked on the page. Turn blocking off for a site you want to support, and it stays off for that site only.
Secure connections first
Brisk upgrades addresses to HTTPS and tells you before it falls back to an unencrypted connection.

Sleeping tabs give memory back

Keep as many tabs open as you like. The ones you're not using step down quietly, and wake up when you click them.

1,921 MB → 375 MB, measured
Four gentle steps
A hidden tab first rests, then uses less memory, then is suspended, and finally is put away entirely, with its scroll position, form text and media position saved for when you come back.
Never at the wrong moment
Tabs playing sound, tabs where you're typing, and sites you add to the never-sleep list are left awake.
A memory budget
When the pages you have open use more than their budget (a quarter of your RAM by default), Brisk puts the longest-hidden tab to sleep first.
Battery saver
On battery power, tabs fall asleep twice as fast.
See what's using what
The task manager (ShiftEsc) shows each tab's memory and processor use, and closes any of them.

Tabs and windows, arranged your way

Brisk works the way you already expect a browser to, with a few things done more carefully.

Tear tabs off
Drag a tab out of the strip to give it a window of its own, or drop it on another window's tabs to move it there. The page moves with it and doesn't reload.
Tab groups
Gather tabs into named, colored groups. Click a group's label to fold it away.
Pinned tabs
Keep the sites you always have open at the start of the strip.
Picks up where you left off
Every window, tab, group and back history comes back when you restart Brisk. Restored tabs load only when you open them.
Reopen what you closed
CtrlShiftT brings back the last tab you closed, and the address bar finds recently closed tabs too.

What you do stays with you

Brisk has no telemetry, no account and no cloud. Everything it knows about you lives in files on your PC.

Private tabs
Private tabs run in their own memory-only space. Nothing they do is kept in history, cookies or the session once they're closed.
Profiles
Give work, home or a shared account its own profile, with separate cookies, sign-ins and site data. Each tab shows whose it is.
Tracking prevention
Choose how strict to be, send the Global Privacy Control signal, and strip click-tracking codes from links.
Site permissions
Camera, microphone, location and notifications ask first by default. Review and reset what each site may do in Settings.
Clear on exit
Have Brisk forget cookies and history every time it closes, or keep history only for as long as you choose.

A password manager you control

Your sign-ins are saved in one encrypted file that only your master password opens.

Strong encryption
Your master password becomes a key through Argon2id, and the whole vault is sealed with XChaCha20-Poly1305. Brisk locks it again after 30 minutes unused.
Fills only where it belongs
A sign-in is offered only on the exact secure site it was saved for, and filled only after you pick it.
Strong passwords on demand
Generate a new password right in the sign-up form.
Bring your passwords along
Import the CSV export from Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox.

Chrome extensions

Add the extensions you rely on straight from the Chrome Web Store.

Install from the store
Open an extension's Web Store page in Brisk and choose Add. You see what the extension can do before it's installed.
Checked before it runs
Every package's signatures are verified against the Web Store's own key, so what you install is exactly what the store published.
Per profile
Turn each extension on for some profiles and off for others. Extensions never run in private tabs.

History, bookmarks and downloads

Everything you've visited, saved and downloaded, in one place (CtrlH).

Search as you type
Find any page by a few letters of its title or address, straight from the address bar. It works for Thai too.
Bookmarks that move with you
Import bookmarks from another browser's HTML export, and export yours as HTML or JSON.
Downloads
Follow downloads as they arrive, and find every file you've downloaded later.

Make it yours

Brisk is built to feel at home on Windows 11.

Light, dark and nine color themes
Follow Windows or choose a look. The new tab page and your pages follow it too.
Your new tab page
Your own shortcuts, a background picture of your choice, and recently closed tabs.
Your search engine
Pick the engine the address bar and new tab page search with.
English and Thai
The whole interface in either language, or following Windows.
Updates built in
Brisk checks for new versions once a day. Each update is signature-checked before it installs, and you choose when to restart.
