A lighter way to browse on Windows.

Brisk blocks ads before they load, puts the tabs you're not using to sleep, and keeps your history on your PC. It's built on the web engine already inside Windows 11, so the whole download is about 7 MB.

Download for Windows 11 Free. Version 0.1.0.

Fast because it does less, not more.

Most of a slow page is things you didn't ask for. Brisk stops them, then gets out of the way.

  • Ads and trackers never load

    Brisk checks every request against EasyList, EasyPrivacy and uBlock Origin's filter lists, and drops ads and trackers before they reach the page. Known malware and phishing sites are stopped with a warning.

    32% fastermedian page load on ten ad-heavy news sites, ad blocking on vs off. How we measured

  • Tabs you're not using sleep

    Hidden tabs step down in stages: hidden, then lighter, then suspended, then put away with their scroll position and form text saved. Click one and it comes back where you left it. Tabs playing audio are never put to sleep.

    1,921 → 375 MBmemory for ten open tabs after ten idle minutes. Details

  • What you do stays on your PC

    No telemetry, no account, no sync, no background service. History, bookmarks and passwords live in files on your computer, and the password vault is encrypted with a key only your master password opens.

    No telemetryBrisk's own traffic is filter lists and the optional daily update check. Nothing about you.

Why Brisk exists

Browsers kept getting heavier. Every one of them shipped its own copy of a web engine, asked for an account, and quietly sent home how it was used. Windows 11 already has a modern engine built in, kept up to date by Windows itself.

Brisk started in November 2026 as a question: what if a browser used that engine and spent its effort on the things that make browsing feel fast — blocking what you didn't ask for and resting what you aren't using — instead of on features nobody requested?

Every performance change to Brisk is measured on real, ad-heavy sites before it ships. If it doesn't make Brisk lighter or faster, it doesn't go in.
